State of preservation of the unit (US sheet, Extended Matrix)

Stato di conservazione dell'unità (scheda US, Extended Matrix) — an Extended Matrix vocabulary module.

An ordinal scale for the STATE OF PRESERVATION box of a stratigraphic unit sheet (ICCD US 2021), gathered from the PyArchInit thesaurus.

Only the labels in the source language come from the sources. Labels marked with * (en, ro, el, es, pl, he, de) were drafted with the module and nobody has verified them yet: usable now, to be corrected after review.

Concepts

1 excellent*

https://w3id.org/extendedmatrix/vocab/us-stato-conservazione/ottimo

The unit survives whole or nearly so, with no alteration that impairs its reading.

ottimo — L'unità è conservata per intero o quasi, senza alterazioni che ne compromettano la lettura.
