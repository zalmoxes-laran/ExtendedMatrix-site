Consistency of the deposit (US sheet, Extended Matrix)

Consistenza del deposito (scheda US, Extended Matrix) — an Extended Matrix vocabulary module.

Terms for the CONSISTENCY box of a stratigraphic unit sheet (ICCD US 2021), gathered from the PyArchInit thesaurus.

Only the labels in the source language come from the sources. Labels marked with * (en, ro, el, es, pl, he, de) were drafted with the module and nobody has verified them yet: usable now, to be corrected after review.

Concepts

https://w3id.org/extendedmatrix/vocab/us-consistenza/compatta

compatta — Il deposito oppone resistenza allo scavo e non si disgrega alla pressione delle dita.
