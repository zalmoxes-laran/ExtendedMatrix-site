Colour of the deposit (US sheet, Extended Matrix)

Colore del deposito (scheda US, Extended Matrix) — an Extended Matrix vocabulary module.

Colour names for the COLOUR box of a stratigraphic unit sheet (ICCD US 2021), gathered from the PyArchInit thesaurus. Names, not Munsell notations: a Munsell reading stays a free annotation.

Only the labels in the source language come from the sources. Labels marked with * (en, ro, el, es, pl, he, de) were drafted with the module and nobody has verified them yet: usable now, to be corrected after review.
